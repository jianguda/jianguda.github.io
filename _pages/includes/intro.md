I was conferred a doctorate in machine learning and software engineering by [Monash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University).
Previously, I was awarded a master's degree in machine learning by [KTH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTH_Royal_Institute_of_Technology) and a bachelor's degree in computer science (elite class) by [SDU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University).

My research investigates the interplay between software engineering and machine learning, especially how engineering principles guide practical analysis and repair techniques to LMs, namely __[LM Analysis and Repair](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10752650)__. I always study LM interpretability as the underlying mechanism, focusing on the inherent semantics, termed as __[LM Semantics]()__.
Proudly share our series of work: [Semantic Basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16184), [Semantic Transition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11753), [Semantic Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24208), [Pseudo-Inverse Tying](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04556), [Semantic Reference Frame](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.32022), ...
